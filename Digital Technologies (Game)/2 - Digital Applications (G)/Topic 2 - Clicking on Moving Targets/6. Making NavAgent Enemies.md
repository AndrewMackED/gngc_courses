# 6. Making NavAgent Enemies

## Step 1 - Fixing a small logic error of mine...
So when we were making the Health script, I thought I was being very clever, and I wasn't far off! But relying on a class called Health to identify if something can take damage is a little flawed. The problem here is that our NavAgent enemies need to inherit their script from CharacterBody3D, can can't inherit from both it AND the Health script. So before we keep working on that script, we need to make a change to our Gun script:

***OLD* gun.gd**
```gdscript
...
if body is Health:
	body.take_damage(1)
...
```

***NEW* gun.gd**
```gdscript
...
if body.has_method("take_damage"):
	body.take_damage(1)
...
```

Now we're looking for anything that has a function called "take_damage", instead of looking for something that specifically is or inherits from the Health class. This means we can add a take_damage function to our Enemy, and it will deal damage to them as well.
